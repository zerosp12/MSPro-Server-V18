// Boss Damage Rank
// Bosses that show a damage ranking while they are fought. The first row that matches the boss, its map and its position is used.
//   Boss: Monster that shows the ranking.
//   Shown to: 0 Players in the zone | 1 Players that hit the boss
//   Map: Map where the boss must be. Any map ignores the map.
//   From X: Zone corner: the smallest column where the boss must stand. * (no limit) ignores this side of the zone.
//   From Y: Zone corner: the smallest row where the boss must stand. * (no limit) ignores this side of the zone.
//   To X: Zone corner: the largest column where the boss must stand. * (no limit) ignores this side of the zone.
//   To Y: Zone corner: the largest row where the boss must stand. * (no limit) ignores this side of the zone.
//   Players in the ranking: How many players the ranking shows, 1 to 10. Anything else becomes 10.
//-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
//  [Number]    [Number]    [Number]    [*/-1~255]    [*/-1~255]    [*/-1~255]    [*/-1~255]    [1~10]
//  Boss        ShowType    Map         X1            Y1            X2            Y2            MaxRank
